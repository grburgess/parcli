# parcli research — 2026-09-13

Deep-research run (5 angles, 22 sources, 108 claims extracted, 25 verified by 3-vote adversarial panel, 18 confirmed, 7 refuted). Verify phase ran on Sonnet/low effort; the ratatui, prior-art, chromiumoxide/fantoccini and TrackingMore/PackageRadar angles were dropped by the claim budget and are **not** covered by verified claims (search-phase leads listed at the bottom).

## Confirmed (high confidence)

- **17TRACK official API** is the recommended backend. `17token` header auth against `api.17track.net`; V1 endpoints `/register`, `/gettrackinfo`, `/gettracklist`, `/changeinfo` (v2.4 base: 3 req/s, 40 numbers per request). OSS client: https://github.com/McSwindler/seventeentrack. Docs: https://www.17track.net/en/api, https://help.17track.net/hc/en-us/articles/37575217580825-Plan-Details
- **Quota**: 1 unit per *registered* tracking number; polling an already-registered number is free. Accounts before 2026-01-07: 100/month recurring. Accounts after: one-time 200 numbers. (https://help.17track.net/hc/en-us/articles/37575160271001-Quota-Deduction-Standards-and-Rules)
- **Ship24 and AfterShip** gate the Tracking API/webhooks behind paid tiers (~$59/mo) — not free API options. (https://www.ship24.com/pricing, https://www.aftership.com/pricing/tracking)
- **Headless scraping is feasible but fragile**: https://github.com/thefuga/parcelsapp-crawler drives `parcelsapp.com/widget` (fill `#track-input`, click `#track-button`) and captures the `api/v2/parcels` XHR via CDP network events. https://github.com/blancleaf/parcel_tracker_cli (ordertracker.com internal JSON) is dead — endpoint returns 404 as of 2026-09-13.
- **ParcelsApp official API v4** (paid): `POST /trackings` → `request_id`, poll `GET /trackings/{id}` until `done`; results retained ~30 min; quota per unique tracking number per cycle; HTTP 402 over-quota, 429 rate-limited. Auth mechanism claim (Bearer) was refuted — check docs. (https://parcelsapp.com/api-docs)

## Confirmed (medium)

- `headless_chrome` and `chromiumoxide` are CDP-direct (no driver binary) with network interception. `headless_chrome` is sync/thread-based; `chromiumoxide` is async. `thirtyfour` (0.37.5, Aug 2026, active) is WebDriver and needs a chromedriver process.

## Refuted

- "17TRACK offers 100 free registrations intended for testing" (superseded by the policy above)
- ordertracker.com endpoint description / rate-limit warning (endpoint gone)
- ParcelsApp v4 Bearer-token auth
- Ship24 API starts at PRO $59/1,000 (exact tier figures not confirmed)
- AfterShip 10 req/s on Premium
